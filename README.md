## Why Whop Video Downloader

Whop delivers course videos as streams, so there is no right-click "Save video", and memberships can
end or content can be removed. Generic download sites cannot see content behind your Whop login.

Whop Video Downloader works inside your signed-in Whop tab, finds the videos, images and text on the page
and saves them to your computer.

## Features

- **Video downloads**: Whop course and lesson videos saved as MP4 in the highest resolution Whop provides
- **Image downloads**: images in posts, lessons and community pages, in their original format
- **Text post export**: lesson text and community posts saved as `.txt`
- **On-player buttons**: download without opening the popup
- **Download Visible**: save every detected item on the page in one click
- **Tabbed popup**: Videos, Images and Text tabs with counts
- **Download queue**: several downloads at once with progress
- **Organized files**: saved to a `Whop` subfolder in your Downloads

## How to Download Whop Videos (Step-by-Step)

1. Install the extension (see [Installation Instructions](#installation-instructions)) and sign in with your email.
2. Sign in to Whop and open the course, product or community page with the video.
3. Click the download button on the player, or click the extension icon and pick the item in the **Videos** tab.
4. Keep the Whop tab open until the download finishes. The file is saved to a `Whop` folder in your Downloads.

## Supported Formats

## Troubleshooting

**A video won't download**  
Make sure you are signed in to Whop with access to that video and keep the tab open. The video may also have expired or been removed by the creator.

**Nothing is detected**  
Refresh the page, let the player load, then click **Rescan** in the popup.

## Installation Instructions

1. Download the ZIP for your browser from the [latest release](https://github.com/serpapps/whop-video-downloader/releases/latest) (for example `whop-downloader-chrome.zip`).
2. Unzip it. You get a folder that contains `manifest.json`.
3. Open your browser's extensions page: `chrome://extensions` (Chrome), `edge://extensions` (Edge), `brave://extensions` (Brave), `opera://extensions` (Opera), `whale://extensions` (Whale) or `browser://extensions` (Yandex).
4. Turn on **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder.
6. Pin the extension, click its icon, and sign in with your email and the one-time code we send you.

Prefer a guided setup? The [product page](https://serp.ly/whop-video-downloader?via=github) walks you through the same steps.
When a newer version is published, the extension shows an update notice; download the new ZIP from
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/serpapps/whop-video-downloader/releases/latest) and load it the same way.

## FAQ

### How do I download videos from Whop?

Open the Whop page with the video, then click the download button on the player or open the extension popup. The video is saved as an MP4 through your browser's normal download flow.

### Can I download a Whop course?

Open each lesson and download its video, or use **Download Visible** to save everything detected on the current page.

### What video quality does it save?

It automatically saves the highest resolution Whop provides for that video.

### Can I download images and text too?

Yes. The popup has separate Videos, Images and Text tabs.

### Do I need to stay signed in to Whop?

Yes. Keep the Whop tab open and signed in while the download runs; your session is needed to fetch the video.

### Where do downloads go?

To a `Whop` subfolder inside your browser's Downloads folder.

### Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale and Yandex on Windows, macOS and Linux. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers are not supported.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get **3 free downloads a week** with no credit card. A **subscription** gives unlimited downloads, with updates included.

## Notes

- Only download content you own or have permission to save.
